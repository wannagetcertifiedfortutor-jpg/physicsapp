Gravitational potential energy and escape speed

U = −GMm/r with zero at infinity, the energy of circular orbits, energy to change orbits, and escape speed.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

With the convention U(∞)=0, a satellite in orbit has U=−rGMm​. What does the negative sign signify?

  1. A mistake — potential energy can never be negative.
  2. Work must be done *on* the satellite to move it to infinity: it is bound, with less energy than a free object at rest far away.
  3. The satellite has negative kinetic energy.
  4. The gravitational force is repulsive at that distance.
