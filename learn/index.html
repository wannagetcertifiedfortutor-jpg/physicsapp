Classical Mechanics: course outline

Practice for MIT OpenCourseWare 8.01SC Classical Mechanics and AP Physics C: Mechanics. Each lesson lists its skills with sample problems. The problems are original practice problems written for this site, not MIT or College Board assignments.

Open the practice app

Review: Vectors

  • Lesson 0: Vectors
    Vectors vs. scalars, vector addition, components, unit vectors, dot and cross products.

Week 1: Kinematics

  • Lesson 1: 1D Kinematics - Position and Velocity
    Coordinate systems and unit vectors in 1D, position and displacement, average and instantaneous velocity as a derivative, velocity from position graphs.
  • Lesson 2: 1D Kinematics - Acceleration
    Acceleration as the derivative of velocity, integration to recover velocity and position, constant acceleration, free fall.
  • Lesson 3: 2D Kinematics
    Position vector in 2D, velocity and acceleration vectors, projectile motion.

Week 2: Newton's Laws

  • Lesson 4: Newton's Laws of Motion
    Newton's first, second and third laws; force as a vector; free-body diagrams.
  • Lesson 5: Gravity
    Universal law of gravitation, gravitational field near Earth's surface, weight.
  • Lesson 6: Contact Forces
    Normal force, static and kinetic friction.
  • Lesson 7: Tension and Springs
    Tension in ropes, Hooke's law, systems of connected objects.

Week 3: Circular Motion

  • Lesson 8: Circular Motion - Position and Velocity
    Polar coordinates, position and velocity in circular motion, angular velocity.
  • Lesson 9: Uniform Circular Motion
    Centripetal acceleration in uniform circular motion.
  • Lesson 10: Circular Motion – Acceleration
    Tangential and radial components of acceleration for non-uniform circular motion.
  • Lesson 11: Newton's 2nd Law and Circular Motion
    Applying Newton's second law to circular motion: banked curves, conical pendulum, orbits.

Week 4: Drag Forces, Constraints and Continuous Systems

  • Lesson 12: Pulleys and Constraints
    Constraint conditions for pulley systems; solving coupled Newton's-law equations.
  • Lesson 13: Massive Rope
    Tension varying along a rope with mass; differential analysis of continuous systems.
  • Lesson 14: Resistive Forces
    Linear and quadratic drag; terminal velocity; solving the equations of motion.

Week 5: Momentum and Impulse

  • Lesson 15: Momentum and Impulse
    Momentum, impulse as the integral of force, Newton's second law in momentum form.
  • Lesson 16: Conservation of Momentum
    Momentum conservation for isolated systems.
  • Lesson 17: Center of Mass and Motion
    Definition of center of mass, center of mass of continuous bodies, motion of the center of mass.

Week 6: Continuous Mass Transfer

  • Lesson 18: Relative Velocity and Recoil
    Relative velocity between frames; recoil problems.
  • Lesson 19: Continuous Mass Transfer
    The rocket equation and systems with changing mass.

Week 7: Kinetic Energy and Work

  • Lesson 20: Kinetic Energy and Work in 1D
    Kinetic energy, work as an integral of force, the work–kinetic-energy theorem in 1D, power.
  • Lesson 21: Kinetic Energy and Work in 2D and 3D
    Scalar product and line integrals for work in more than one dimension.
  • Lesson 22: Conservative and Non-Conservative Forces
    Path independence, conservative forces, friction as a non-conservative force.

Week 8: Potential Energy and Energy Conservation

  • Lesson 23: Potential Energy
    Potential energy functions for gravity (near-Earth and universal) and springs.
  • Lesson 24: Conservation of Energy
    Mechanical energy conservation and the general energy principle with non-conservative work.
  • Lesson 25: Potential Energy Diagrams
    Force as the negative derivative of potential; equilibrium points; turning points.

Week 9: Collision Theory

  • Lesson 26: Types of Collision
    Elastic, inelastic and perfectly inelastic collisions; momentum and energy bookkeeping.
  • Lesson 27: Elastic Collisions
    Solving 1D elastic collisions; relative velocity reversal.
  • Deep Dive: Center of Mass Reference Frame
    Analyzing collisions in the center-of-mass frame (enrichment).

Week 10: Rotational Motion

  • Lesson 28: Motion of a Rigid Body
    Rigid bodies; rotational kinematics about a fixed axis.
  • Lesson 29: Moment of Inertia
    Rotational kinetic energy; moment of inertia by integration; parallel-axis theorem.
  • Lesson 30: Torque
    Torque as r × F; static equilibrium.
  • Lesson 31: Rotational Dynamics
    τ = Iα; rotational work and energy; massive pulleys.

Week 11: Angular Momentum

  • Lesson 32: Angular Momentum of a Point Particle
    Angular momentum L = r × p about a point.
  • Lesson 33: Angular Momentum of a Rigid Body
    L = Iω for rigid bodies about a fixed axis.
  • Lesson 34: Torque and Angular Impulse
    Torque as the rate of change of angular momentum; angular impulse.

Week 12: Rotations and Translation - Rolling

  • Lesson 35: Rolling Kinematics
    Rolling without slipping: v_cm = Rω; velocities of points on a wheel.
  • Lesson 36: Rolling Dynamics
    Friction on rolling objects; rolling down inclines.
  • Lesson 37: Rolling Kinetic Energy & Angular Momentum
    Kinetic energy of translation plus rotation; angular momentum of rolling bodies.
  • Deep Dive: Gyroscopes
    Gyroscopic precession (enrichment).
