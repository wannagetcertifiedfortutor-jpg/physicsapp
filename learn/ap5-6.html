Sample problem · AP Free Response · Derive

A uniform solid disk of mass M and radius R is mounted on a frictionless axle through its center. A string wrapped around its rim is pulled with constant tension F. Derive the disk's angular acceleration α in terms of F, M and R. (Use I=21​MR2.)

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A 1 kg block hangs from a light string wrapped around a uniform solid cylinder (pulley) of mass 6 kg and radius 0.1 m, free to turn on a frictionless horizontal axle. The block is released from rest and the string does not slip. Use g=9.8 m/s² and I=21​MR2.
