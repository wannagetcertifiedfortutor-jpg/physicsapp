Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A simple pendulum of length 0.5 m swings with a small amplitude near Earth's surface. What is its period? Use g=9.8 m/s².

  1. 27.8 s
  2. 0.71 s
  3. 0.226 s
  4. 1.42 s
Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A simple pendulum swings with a small amplitude. Then its bob is replaced by one with twice the mass, with everything else unchanged. How does its period change?

  1. The period is halved.
  2. The period increases by a factor of 2​.
  3. The period doubles.
  4. The period is unchanged.
