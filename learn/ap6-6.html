Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

Satellite A orbits Earth in a circle of radius r and satellite B in a circle of radius 4r. How do their orbital speeds compare?

  1. vB​=2vA​
  2. vB​=vA​ — orbital speed does not depend on radius
  3. vB​=vA​/4
  4. vB​=vA​/2
