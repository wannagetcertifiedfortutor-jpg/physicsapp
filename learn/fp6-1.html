Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

Three small 0.5 kg beads are fixed to a light rod of length 1 m: one at each end and one at the midpoint. Find the moment of inertia of the beads about an axis perpendicular to the rod through the middle mass.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A uniform rod of mass 1 kg and length 0.5 m pivots about one end. A small 0.9 kg ball is fixed to the other end. What is the rotational inertia about the pivot?

  1. 0.475 kg·m²
  2. 0.533 kg·m²
  3. 0.246 kg·m²
  4. 0.308 kg·m²
