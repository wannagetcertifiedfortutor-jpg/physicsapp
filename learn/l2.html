Acceleration as a derivative

Differentiate v(t) or x(t) twice; interpret the sign of acceleration relative to velocity.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A car moves in the −x direction and is slowing down. Which pair of signs is correct?

  1. vx​>0 and ax​>0
  2. vx​<0 and ax​>0
  3. vx​>0 and ax​<0
  4. vx​<0 and ax​<0
Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A drone's altitude is y(t)=0.5t3−3t2 (SI units, +y up). Find its acceleration at t=2 s.

Integrating acceleration to velocity and position

Use definite integrals and initial conditions to obtain v(t) and x(t) from a(t), including non-constant acceleration.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A sled on ice has acceleration ax​(t)=0.5t (SI units) and velocity vx​(0)=4 m/s. Find vx​ at t=2 s.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Derive

A rocket sled starts from rest at t=0. For 0≤t≤T its acceleration decreases linearly: ax​(t)=a0​(1−Tt​). Derive vx​(t) for 0≤t≤T in terms of a0​, T and t.

Constant acceleration and free fall

Derive and apply the constant-acceleration relations, including vertical motion under gravity.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A car traveling at 20 m/s brakes with constant deceleration of magnitude 4 m/s². How far does it travel before stopping?

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Derive

A ball is launched straight up with speed v0​ from height h0​ (+y up, constant g, no air resistance). Derive the maximum height ymax​ above the ground in terms of v0​, g and h0​.

Velocity and acceleration graphs

Connect a(t), v(t) and x(t) graphs: slopes and areas.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Analyze the Graph

The graph shows ax​(t) for an object with vx​(0)=5 m/s. What is vx​ at t=4 s?

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Analyze the Graph

The graph shows vx​(t) for a cart. What is the cart's acceleration between t=3 s and t=5 s?
