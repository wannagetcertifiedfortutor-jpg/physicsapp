Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A car skids to a stop on a level road with its wheels locked. If the car's initial speed were 2 times greater (same road, same car), how would the skidding distance compare?

  1. 2 times as far, because the friction force is also 2 times larger at higher speed.
  2. The same distance, because friction removes a fixed amount of energy per unit mass.
  3. 2 times as far, because stopping distance is proportional to speed.
  4. 4 times as far, because the kinetic energy to be removed is 4 times larger while the friction force is unchanged.
