Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

You walk forward at constant velocity on a level floor. During a step, your foot pushes on the floor. Which way does the floor's friction force on your foot point?

  1. Zero on average, since your velocity is constant.
  2. Upward, since it must support your weight.
  3. Backward, because friction always opposes motion.
  4. Forward, opposite to the backward push of the foot on the floor.
Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A 20 kg crate rests on a level floor with μs​=0.6 and μk​=0.5. A horizontal force of 82.3 N is applied and the crate does not move. What is the magnitude of the friction force acting on the crate? Take g=9.8 m/s².
