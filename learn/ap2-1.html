  1. It is the point where gravity acts most strongly.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A firework shell follows a parabolic path and explodes in mid-air into many fragments. Neglecting air resistance, immediately after the explosion the center of mass of the fragments

  1. falls straight down from the point of explosion.
  2. follows a new parabola determined by the largest fragment.
  3. stops moving because the fragments fly in all directions.
  4. continues along the original parabola as if nothing had happened.
