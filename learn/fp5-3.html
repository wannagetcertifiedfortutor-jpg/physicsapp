Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

Moving at constant speed, a seat on a carousel goes around a circle of radius 2.5 m once every 5 s. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

  1. 0.628 m/s²
  2. 3.14 m/s²
  3. 3.95 m/s²
  4. 19.7 m/s²
Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A stone whirls on a string at constant speed v in a horizontal circle of radius R. What is the stone's acceleration?

  1. Away from the center, with magnitude v2/R (the 'centrifugal' acceleration).
  2. Toward the center of the circle, with magnitude v2/R.
  3. Along the velocity, with magnitude v2/R.
  4. Zero, because the speed is constant.
