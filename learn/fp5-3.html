Centripetal acceleration

Derive and apply a = v²/r = ω²r and the direction of acceleration in uniform circular motion.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

Moving at constant speed, a stone whirled on a string goes around a circle of radius 1.4 m once every 0.7 s. What is the magnitude of its acceleration?

  1. 18 m/s²
  2. 12.6 m/s²
  3. 113 m/s²
  4. 79 m/s²
Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A stone whirls on a string at constant speed v in a horizontal circle of radius R. What is the stone's acceleration?

  1. Away from the center, with magnitude v2/R (the 'centrifugal' acceleration).
  2. Toward the center of the circle, with magnitude v2/R.
  3. Along the velocity, with magnitude v2/R.
  4. Zero, because the speed is constant.

Tangential and radial acceleration

Combine tangential (rα) and radial (v²/r) acceleration for objects speeding up or slowing down on a circle.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A car drives around a circular track while slowing down. Which statement about the direction of its acceleration vector is correct?

  1. It points directly opposite to the velocity.
  2. It points inward and forward: the angle between a and v is less than 90∘.
  3. It points exactly toward the center of the circle.
  4. It points inward and backward: the angle between a and v is greater than 90∘.
Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A particle moves on a circle of radius 2 m. At one instant its speed is 6 m/s and its speed is increasing at 1.5 m/s². What is the radial (centripetal) component of its acceleration at this instant?

Newton's second law in circular motion

Identify the forces providing centripetal acceleration and solve for speeds, tensions and normal forces.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A car rounds a flat curve of radius 60 m on dry asphalt, where the coefficient of static friction between tires and road is 0.7. What is the greatest speed at which it can take the curve without skidding?

  1. 14.3 m/s
  2. 412 m/s
  3. 20.3 m/s
  4. 24.2 m/s

  1. 42 m/s
