Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A gyroscope's axle points horizontally away from its pivot along +x, and the flywheel spins so that its angular momentum L also points along +x. Gravity acts along −z (down). In which direction does the free end of the axle start to move?

  1. Toward −y: the axle precesses horizontally, clockwise seen from above.
  2. Toward +z: the free end rises.
  3. Toward −z: the free end falls.
  4. Toward +y: the axle precesses horizontally, counter-clockwise seen from above.
Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A spinning gyroscope is supported at one end of its axle, which is horizontal, and it precesses slowly about a vertical axis. The gyroscope is spun up again so that its spin angular speed is doubled, with everything else unchanged. What happens to the angular speed of precession?

  1. It is unchanged.
  2. It is divided by 4.
  3. It is multiplied by 2.
  4. It is divided by 2.
