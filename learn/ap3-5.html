Power

Compute instantaneous and average power as dW/dt and F·v.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A ball is thrown at an angle and follows a parabolic arc (no drag). At the highest point of its flight, the instantaneous power delivered by gravity to the ball is …

  1. −mgv, where v is the ball's speed there, because gravity opposes the motion.
  2. mgv, where v is the ball's speed there.
  3. zero, because the ball is momentarily at rest.
  4. zero, because the velocity is horizontal and perpendicular to the gravitational force.
