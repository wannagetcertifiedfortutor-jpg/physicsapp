Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

Three small 0.5 kg beads are fixed to a light rod of length 1 m: one at each end and one at the midpoint. Find the moment of inertia of the beads about an axis perpendicular to the rod through the middle mass.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A solid disk and a thin ring have the same mass M and the same radius R. Both spin about their symmetry axes at the same ω. Which is true?

  1. The disk has the larger moment of inertia because it contains more material.
  2. The ring has twice the moment of inertia but the same kinetic energy, since ω is the same.
  3. They have the same moment of inertia because mass and radius are equal.
  4. The ring has twice the moment of inertia and twice the rotational kinetic energy of the disk.
