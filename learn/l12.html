Pulley constraint conditions

Write string-length constraints and differentiate to relate accelerations.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

Two blocks hang from the ends of a single string over a fixed pulley (an Atwood machine). Measure each block's position y1​, y2​ downward from the pulley axle. Which relation follows from the string having fixed length?

  1. a1​=2a2​
  2. a1​+a2​=g
  3. a1​=a2​
  4. a1​=−a2​
Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

In a system with one movable pulley, the string constraint is 2y1​+y2​=const (both measured downward). Block 2 is descending at 0.6 m/s. What is the speed of block 1 and in which direction is it moving? Enter the speed.

Solving pulley systems

Combine free-body diagrams with constraints to find accelerations and tensions.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

Masses 4 kg and 2.5 kg hang from an ideal Atwood machine. Find the magnitude of their acceleration (g=9.8 m/s²).

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Derive

Two blocks of masses m1​ and m2​ hang from a massless string over a frictionless, massless pulley (an Atwood machine). Derive the magnitude of the acceleration a of the blocks (assume m1​>m2​), in terms of m1​, m2​ and g.
