Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A book rests on a table. The table pushes up on the book with a normal force. What is the Newton's-third-law partner of this force?

  1. The table pushes down on the floor.
  2. There is no partner because the book is in equilibrium.
  3. The Earth pulls down on the book (the book's weight).
  4. The book pushes down on the table with a force of equal magnitude.
Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate
