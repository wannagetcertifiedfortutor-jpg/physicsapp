Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A rocket engine ejects exhaust at 1800 m/s relative to the rocket, burning fuel at a steady 20 kg/s. The rocket's mass at this instant is 6000 kg. What is the thrust (the force the exhaust exerts on the rocket)?

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

Sand pours vertically from a hopper onto a horizontal conveyor belt at a steady 3 kg/s. The belt moves at a constant 3 m/s. What horizontal force must the motor exert on the belt to keep it moving at constant speed? (Ignore friction in the bearings.)
