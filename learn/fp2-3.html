Normal force

Use Newton's laws to find the normal force on level surfaces, on inclines, with angled pushes and pulls, and when the object accelerates.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

Which statement best describes the normal force between two surfaces in contact?

  1. It is always vertical, because it supports the object against gravity.
  2. It takes whatever value keeps the two surfaces from passing through each other.
  3. It is the reaction force to gravity, required by Newton's third law.
  4. It always equals the object's weight mg.
Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A 3 kg block rests on a frictionless incline of angle 45∘ (held momentarily at rest by a small side stop that exerts no force perpendicular to the surface). What is the normal force the incline exerts on the block? Take g=9.8 m/s².

Apparent weight

The scale reading, or normal force, on a person or object that is accelerating vertically, including free fall.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A 48 kg student stands on a bathroom scale inside an elevator whose cable snaps, so the elevator and student fall freely (air resistance negligible). What does the scale read while they fall?

  1. 941 N, because the elevator is accelerating.
  2. 48 N, because a scale measures mass.
  3. 470 N, because gravity on the student is unchanged.
  4. 0 N, because the student and the scale accelerate together at g, so the scale does not need to push on the student.
Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A 48 kg student stands in an elevator that is speeding up going up with an acceleration of magnitude 2.5 m/s². What is the normal force from the floor on the student?

  1. 120 N
  2. 470 N
  3. 590 N
  4. 350 N
