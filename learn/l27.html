Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A 2 kg glider moving at 5 m/s in the +x direction on an air track collides elastically, head-on, with a 1 kg glider at rest. Find the x-component of the velocity of the incoming 2 kg glider after the collision (include the sign).

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

Two balls approach each other head-on along a line: ball A at 3 m/s to the right, ball B at 4 m/s to the left. They collide elastically. What is their relative speed (rate of separation) just after the collision?

  1. 0 — after the collision the balls move together.
  2. It depends on the masses, which are not given.
  3. 1 m/s — the difference of the speeds.
  4. 7 m/s — the same relative speed as before, with the balls now separating.
