Potential energy

Gravitational potential energy near Earth (mgΔy) and elastic potential energy (½kx²) of a system, and why only changes in potential energy matter.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A 17.5 kg backpack is carried from 18 m to 27 m above the ground. What is the change in the gravitational potential energy of the object–Earth system? Use g=9.8 m/s².

  1. 158 J
  2. −1540 J
  3. 1540 J
  4. 4630 J
Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

Two students analyze the same ball thrown upward. One sets Ug​=0 at the ground, the other at the launch height. Which quantities will they disagree on?

  1. Nothing — the potential energy itself is the same for both students.
  2. Only the value of Ug​ at any given instant; ΔUg​, ΔK and the speed at every point agree.
  3. The maximum height reached, since it depends on how much potential energy is available.
  4. The speed at the top, because the student with the lower zero level assigns more potential energy there.

Conservation of mechanical energy
