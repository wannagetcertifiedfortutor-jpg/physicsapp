Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

Two students analyze the same ball thrown upward. One sets Ug​=0 at the ground, the other at the launch height. Which quantities will they disagree on?

  1. The maximum height reached, since it depends on how much potential energy is available.
  2. Nothing — the potential energy itself is the same for both students.
  3. The speed at the top, because the student with the lower zero level assigns more potential energy there.
  4. Only the value of Ug​ at any given instant; ΔUg​, ΔK and the speed at every point agree.
Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A 2 kg book sits on a shelf 2 m above the floor. A table top is 1.5 m above the floor. Taking the gravitational potential energy to be zero at the table top (which is 1.5 m above the floor), what is the book's potential energy U? Use g=9.8 m/s².
