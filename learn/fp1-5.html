Free fall

Objects moving only under gravity near Earth's surface: launched up, dropped, and thrown from a height.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A ball is thrown straight up and air resistance is negligible. Which statement describes the ball at the instant it reaches its highest point?

  1. The velocity is 9.8 m/s downward and the acceleration is zero.
  2. The velocity is zero and the acceleration is 9.8 m/s² downward.
  3. The velocity is zero and the acceleration is 9.8 m/s² upward.
  4. The velocity and the acceleration are both zero.
