Rolling dynamics

Derive the acceleration of a rolling body on an incline and the required friction.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A solid sphere is released from rest on an incline of 37∘ and rolls without slipping. What is the magnitude of the acceleration of its center of mass? (Use g=9.8 m/s².)

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A ball rolls without slipping up an incline, slowing down. Which way does the static friction force on it point?

  1. There is no friction, because the ball is not slipping.
  2. Perpendicular to the incline.
  3. Down the incline, opposing the ball's upward motion.
  4. Up the incline — friction must produce the torque that slows the ball's spin as it slows its climb.
