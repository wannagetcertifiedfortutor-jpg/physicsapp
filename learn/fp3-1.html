Work in 1D

Compute work by integrating a position-dependent force; interpret area under F(x).

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Analyze the Graph

The graph shows the x-component of the net force on a 2 kg cart as it moves along the +x axis. How much work does this force do on the cart between x=0 and x=6 m?

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

You lower a heavy box slowly at constant speed from a shelf to the floor, holding it the whole way. During the descent, which statement about the work done is correct?

  1. Your hands do positive work because you are exerting an upward force.
  2. Neither force does work because the speed does not change.
  3. Gravity does negative work because the box ends up lower.
  4. Gravity does positive work and your hands do negative work; the two cancel.

Work as a line integral

Evaluate W = ∫F·dr along specified paths; use the scalar product.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A block slides down a rough incline. Which of the following forces does zero work on the block during the slide?

  1. Gravity, because it is vertical while the block moves along the slope.
  2. No force does zero work — every force acting on a moving object does work.
  3. Friction, because the block does not stop.
  4. The normal force, because it is perpendicular to the displacement at every instant.
