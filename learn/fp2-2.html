Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A worker pulls a crate with a rope at 37∘ above the horizontal with a force of 120 N, and it slides across the floor at a constant velocity. What is the magnitude of the friction force on it?

  1. 0 N
  2. 120 N
  3. 95.8 N
  4. 72.2 N
Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

During a collision, a 1250 kg car exerts a force of 6000 N on a 8750 kg truck. What is the magnitude of the car's acceleration at that moment, from this force?

  1. 0.6 m/s²
  2. 33.6 m/s²
  3. 0.686 m/s²
  4. 4.8 m/s²

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A block of mass 18.5 kg slides down a frictionless ramp inclined at 20∘. From its free-body diagram, what is the block's acceleration? Use g=9.8 m/s².

  1. 9.8 m/s²
  2. 3.57 m/s²
  3. 9.21 m/s²
  4. 3.35 m/s²
Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A rope pulls straight up on a 9 kg bucket with a tension of 123 N. Taking up as positive, what is the bucket's acceleration?

  1. −3.87 m/s²
  2. 13.7 m/s²
  3. 3.87 m/s²
  4. 23.5 m/s²
