Compute L about a point for straight-line and circular motion; dependence on the chosen point.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A 0.5 kg ball on a string moves in a horizontal circle of radius 0.5 m with period 1 s. Find the magnitude of its angular momentum about the center of the circle.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A puck slides in a straight line across frictionless ice at constant velocity. About a point P that is not on its path, its angular momentum is …

  1. increasing in magnitude as the puck moves away from P.
  2. largest when the puck is closest to P.
  3. zero, because the puck is not rotating about P.
  4. constant in time and non-zero.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

  1. 72 rad/s
  2. 6 rad/s
  3. 24 rad/s
  4. 48 rad/s
