Position, displacement and coordinate choice

Distinguish position, displacement and distance traveled in a chosen 1D coordinate system.

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

  1. The elevator's velocity is −3 m/s and its position coordinate increases with time.
  2. The sign of velocity cannot be determined without knowing the acceleration.
  3. The elevator's velocity is +3 m/s because speed is always positive.
  4. The elevator's velocity is −3 m/s; its position coordinate decreases with time.
Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A runner on a straight track is at x=−2 m, jogs to x=4 m, then turns and stops at x=−3 m. What is the runner's displacement for the whole trip? (Give the sign for displacement.)

Average velocity

Compute average velocity over an interval from a position function or a position graph, and distinguish it from average speed.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

A particle's position is x(t)=4t+1.5t2 (SI units). Find its average velocity between t=1 s and t=4 s.

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Analyze the Graph

The graph shows x(t) for a cart. Find the cart's average velocity between t=0 s and t=4 s. (Hover the curve to read values.)

Instantaneous velocity as a derivative

Differentiate position functions to obtain v(t); read velocity as the slope of x(t).

Sample problem · AP Free Response · Calculate

The position of a bead is x(t)=0.5t3−6t (SI units). What is its velocity at t=1.5 s?

Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A particle has position x(t)=2(t−3)2−3 (SI units). When is its velocity zero?

  1. Never — the particle is always moving because x changes with t.
  2. At the time when x=0.
  3. At t=3 s, where the slope of x(t) is zero.
  4. At t=0, because the particle starts from rest.
