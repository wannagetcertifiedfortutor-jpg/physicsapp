Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

  1. Along −^, the direction of the position vector
  2. −k^ (into the page; the body tends to rotate clockwise)
  3. +k^ (out of the page; the body tends to rotate counter-clockwise)
  4. Along +^​, the direction of the force
