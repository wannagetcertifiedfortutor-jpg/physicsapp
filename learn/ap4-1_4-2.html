Sample problem · AP Multiple Choice

A rubber ball and a clay ball of equal mass are thrown at a wall with equal speeds. The rubber ball bounces back; the clay ball sticks. Which receives the larger-magnitude impulse from the wall?

  1. They are equal — same mass, same speed.
  2. It depends on which one is in contact longer.
  3. The clay ball — it stops completely.
  4. The rubber ball — its momentum changes by about 2mv rather than mv.
