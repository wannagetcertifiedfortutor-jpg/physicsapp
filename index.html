AP Physics C: Mechanics Practice

An educational practice site for AP Physics C: Mechanics. It follows the seven units of the College Board course, with original multiple-choice practice problems, hints and worked solutions.

Units

  • Unit 1: Kinematics
  • Unit 2: Force and Translational Dynamics
  • Unit 3: Work, Energy, and Power
  • Unit 4: Linear Momentum
  • Unit 5: Torque and Rotational Dynamics
  • Unit 6: Energy and Momentum of Rotating Systems
  • Unit 7: Oscillations

How it works

Students answer numerical, symbolic and multiple-choice questions. Answers are checked for algebraic equivalence, significant figures and units, and each skill moves from Familiar to Proficient to Mastered.
