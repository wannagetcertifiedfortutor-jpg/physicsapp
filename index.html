AP Physics C: Mechanics Practice

An educational practice site for AP Physics C: Mechanics. It follows the Flipping Physics AP Physics C: Mechanics sequence, checked against every College Board AP topic, with original multiple-choice practice problems, hints and worked solutions.

Units

  • Unit 1: Kinematics
  • Unit 2: Dynamics: Newton's Laws and Forces
  • Unit 3: Work, Energy, Power and Spring Force
  • Unit 4: Momentum, Impulse and Center of Mass
  • Unit 5: Rotational Kinematics
  • Unit 6: Rotational Dynamics
  • Unit 7: Universal Gravitation and Kepler's Laws
  • Unit 8: Simple Harmonic Motion

How it works

Students answer numerical, symbolic and multiple-choice questions. Answers are checked for algebraic equivalence, significant figures and units, and each skill moves from Familiar to Proficient to Mastered.
