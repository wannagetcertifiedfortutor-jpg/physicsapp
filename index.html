8.01SC Practice: Classical Mechanics

An educational practice site for introductory physics. It follows MIT OpenCourseWare 8.01SC Classical Mechanics and the College Board AP Physics C: Mechanics course, with original practice problems, hints and worked solutions.

Topics

  • Vectors and one- and two-dimensional kinematics; projectile motion
  • Newton's laws of motion, friction, circular motion and drag
  • Work, kinetic and potential energy, and conservation of energy
  • Momentum, impulse, collisions and center of mass
  • Rotational motion, torque, angular momentum and rolling
  • Gravitation, orbits and simple harmonic motion

How it works

Students answer numerical, symbolic and multiple-choice questions. Answers are checked for algebraic equivalence, significant figures and units, and each skill moves from Familiar to Proficient to Mastered.
